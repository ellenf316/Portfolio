About

Hi! I'm Ellen, an AI conversation designer, content designer, and content strategist. Over the past several years, I've shaped how people interact with AI and digital products at companies including Microsoft, Expedia, Allstate, and Innovaccer, working across conversation design, prompt engineering, LLM evaluation, UX writing, and content strategy.

I've worked everywhere from large, complex organizations to startups, with a particular strength in making complicated and regulated systems understandable. I'm especially drawn to healthcare, where thoughtful content and AI have the potential to make a meaningful difference in people's lives.
