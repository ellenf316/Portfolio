Allstate Small Business Auto Insurance

Building the voice, tone, and end-to-end content system for Midway — Allstate's first auto insurance product built specifically for small businesses with four or fewer vehicles.

Allstate
Product

Challenge

Allstate sought a unique niche in the commercial auto insurance marketplace: a new auto insurance product, internally called Midway, built specifically for small businesses with four or fewer vehicles — a segment that didn't fit neatly into existing commercial or personal auto products.

Voice & Tone Guidelines

Before writing any product copy, I ran a voice and tone workshop to get the team aligned on how Midway should sound — and, just as importantly, how it shouldn't. We defined six voice characteristics, each paired with the trap it was meant to avoid:

Voice Characteristic Not This Not This
Advising Opinionated / Know-It-All Here's a policy option that looks like it might fit your needs. You should purchase this policy because it is exactly what you need.
Simple Complicated Your bodily injury liability coverage means insurance will pay up to $20,000 per person and $40,000 for injuries to other people involved in the accident. The bodily injury liability coverage on your policy is $20,000 per person and $40,000 per accident.
Trustworthy Snake oils / Salesy / Pompous We are one of the most experienced companies in this business and want to help you find the right fit for your needs. We are the best and only commercial auto insurance that will work for you.
Informative Know-it-all Other businesses similar to yours often choose this policy. This is the policy that is best for you.
Reliable All-over-the-place Your claim is being taken care of. We'll email you with an update within 24 hours. We're taking a look at your claim and aren't sure when it will be handled.
Conversational Corporate-speak or formal You've successfully updated your auto insurance policy for your small business. You made an endorsement to your commercial vehicle fleet insurance policy.

We also mapped Midway's tone across a set of sliding scales (based on the Nielsen Norman and Big Brand Scale models), to give the team a shared, visual reference for exactly how funny, formal, or upscale the brand should read:

Before & After: Telematics Activation Email

This reminder email nudges customers to activate Drivewise, Allstate's telematics feature, after they've already purchased a policy. The before version buries the ask in a wall of paragraphs; the after version ties the feature to its real brand name and breaks activation into two numbered steps.

Before

Subject: Allstate: Complete the Telematics program activation process

Hello <Name>,

When you purchased your Allstate Business Insurance policy, you signed up for our safe driving Telematics program, initiated through the Routely app. It appears you still need to activate Routely. You should have received a text message with an activation code and a link to download the app when you purchased your policy.

Please use this code to activate Routely today so that you can begin participating in the program and better understand your driving habits.

If you need support with downloading the Routely app or with activating your account, please contact us at 1-800-659-3144 or smallbusinessinsurance@allstate.com. The activation code expires after 30 days.

After

Subject: Allstate: Activate Drivewise® to keep saving

Hello <Name>,

Welcome to Allstate's small business auto insurance with Drivewise®. As a reminder, you still need to activate Drivewise®, the feature you signed up for when you purchased your policy. Drivewise® helps you drive safer and keep your costs low.

Setting up Drivewise® is easy and will just take a few minutes.

1. Download the Routely app: <iOS badge> <Google Play badge>
2. Open the Routely app, and use this activation code to set up Drivewise: <numeric code>

Once you're set up, all you have to do is let the Routely app run in the background to start recording your trips. Set up Drivewise® today!

Before & After: Legal Language

Insurance disclosures are legally required, but they don't have to be unreadable. The original credit-report notice ran as a single unbroken paragraph in dense, all-caps legalese:

For your information, Allstate may order personal credit reports (except in HI and NY), motor vehicle reports, loss information reports and/or other financial reports in conjunction with your application… loss information reports are ordered on named insureds only. If the named insured is an individual or a sole proprietor, we will order a personal credit report (except in HI and NY) and loss information reports on that individual…

The rewrite kept every legally required disclosure but restructured it into labeled sections with a scannable contact block for each reporting agency:

What We Collect

For your information, Allstate may order commercial and/or personal credit reports (except in HI and NY), motor vehicle reports and/or other financial reports in conjunction with your application for insurance. A third party may be used to calculate the insurance score of the company and/or business owner. This information, along with the information you provide, will be shared with outside parties that perform services on Allstate's behalf. View Privacy Policy

Before & After: Agent-Facing Language

Insurance agents and licensed sales producers (LSPs) used a separate, B2B version of the flow. The original reference doc for agents was internal shorthand — accurate, but not written in sentences an agent could read aloud to a customer on a call. I rewrote it as full sales-coaching language:

Before (internal shorthand) After (agent-facing coaching)
Link to appetite for the product Requirements for customers: 4 vehicles or less, with less than 15,000 lbs gross vehicle weight for each; not being used for rideshare, transportation of people/livery; they need to have prior insurance unless their business is new.
Reminder for Form Accuracy: Agents/LSPs would benefit from knowing that if they enter fake/placeholder information early in the form, that entry will impact the downstream price estimate. Please make sure to enter real information to ensure that the pricing you give the customer is as accurate as possible.
Tip: Policy needs to start between today and 60 days from today If the customer needs to start their policy more than 60 days from today, you can continue the quoting process but let them know you can reach out to them to complete it closer to when they need the policy to start.
If they are an existing Allstate customer, ask if they're ok using their existing data to start the quote If they are an existing Allstate customer, ask them if it's okay for us to prefill their quote with information we already have about them. Assure the customer that they have control over how we handle their existing information.
