About

Hi! I'm Ellen, an AI conversation designer, content designer, and content strategist working at the intersection of language and AI. Over the past several years, I've shaped how people interact with AI and digital products at companies including Microsoft, Expedia, Allstate, and Innovaccer, spanning conversation design, prompt engineering, LLM evaluation, and UX writing.

My work spans big legacy corporations to startups, with a particular strength in making complex, regulated systems understandable. I'm especially drawn to healthcare, where I get to work on products that genuinely improve people's lives. I'm also an instructor for UX Content Collective, where I help train new and experienced UX writers.

Outside of work, you'll find me with my rescue dogs, reading, writing and performing comedy, sailing, or doing yoga.

I want to work with good people on good products that improve lives, and I'm especially excited about how AI can drive both efficiency and better outcomes in and outside healthcare.
